ALPR cameras

Automated license plate readers (mostly Flock Safety) mapped from OpenStreetMap / DeFlock community data.

What they capture

  • Plate, make, model, color
  • Time and exact location
  • Vehicle features (stickers, damage)

Network

  • ~30-day default retention
  • Many agencies share data nationally
  • 100k+ cameras claimed by Flock
