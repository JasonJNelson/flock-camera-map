What are these cameras?

This map shows automated license plate readers (ALPRs) — mostly Flock Safety devices — sourced from the same OpenStreetMap / DeFlock data used by FlockHopper.

What they capture

  • License plate number
  • Make, model, color, body type
  • Distinctive features (stickers, racks, damage)
  • Exact time and location of every pass

No facial recognition. Focus is on the vehicle.

How the network works

  • Installed by police, HOAs, businesses, and schools
  • Default retention ~30 days (agencies can extend)
  • Many agencies share data nationwide
  • Flock claims 120k+ cameras across 49 states

About this map

Camera locations come from community-mapped OpenStreetMap nodes (surveillance:type=ALPR), served as vector tiles by the FlockHopper / dontgetflocked pipeline. Coverage is incomplete and volunteer-maintained.
